# Process Time Ordering Diagram

**Question:** A ________________ shows the time ordering of processes and identifies relationships between processes.

**Options:**
a. DFD
b. State Transition Diagram
c. Flowcharts
d. Gantt Chart

**Correct Answer:** d. Gantt Chart

**Explanation:**
A Gantt Chart shows the time ordering (schedule) of processes/tasks and can indicate relationships/dependencies between them.
